# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people.
- Assume competence: ask why a decision was made before rewriting it.
- Give reproducible reports and actionable review comments.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a feature to keep the surface small.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised content or attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument.
- Credential dumping: never paste tokens, keys, or session transcripts into an issue.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately. Responses may range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.
Maintainers who violate this policy face the same consequences as anyone else.
